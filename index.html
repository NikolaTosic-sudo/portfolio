Never stop Learning

Something I love about this career is that there's always something new to learn.

There's an old saying: "Jack of all trades, master of none". But that's only part of it. The full version is actually: "Jack of all trades, master of one". And I believe that's exactly how it should be - stay curious, explore broadly, but master at least one thing deeply.

I'm always driven to improve, to explore new technologies, and to bring fresh ideas to the table - whatever they may be.

One of the best ways to grow is by working with others. Even a complete beginner can offer a new perspective. And experienced developers? They’re invaluable — not just for sharing technical knowledge, but for mentoring others in how to think, collaborate, and write clean, respectful code.

My journey as a Developer

I discovered my passion for coding after getting bored with only playing video games on my computer - coding is a better game . After playing around with various projects in React/Next.js I got some serious experience in the stack, and now with 4+ years of professional work under my belt, I'm comfortable and confident to adapt to any environment and contribute meaningfully from day one.

I recently built and completed a full-featured chess application using Go, HTMX, and PostgreSQL, with a strong focus on backend-driven design.

I believe that continuously learning and challenging myself is what truly defines a great developer.
